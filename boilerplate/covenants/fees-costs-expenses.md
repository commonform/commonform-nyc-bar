# Fees, Costs and Expenses
Except as expressly provided otherwise in this _Agreement_ or any other _Transaction Document_, all fees, costs and expenses incurred in connection with this _Agreement_ and the other _Transaction Documents_ and the transactions contemplated hereby and thereby (collectively, the **Transactions**) shall be paid by the party incurring such fees, costs and expenses, whether or not the _Transactions_ are consummated. In the event of termination of this _Agreement_ or any other _Transaction Document_, the obligation of each party to pay its own fees, costs and expenses will be subject to any rights of such party arising from a breach of this _Agreement_ or any other _Transaction Document_ by any other party.
